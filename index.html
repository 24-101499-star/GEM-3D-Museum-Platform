Royal Amulet

The WingedScarabProtection

The sacred scarab beetle, known as Khepri in ancient Egypt, symbolized renewal and resurrection. This winged amulet from Tutankhamun's tomb was crafted from gold, lapis lazuli, and turquoise. Placed on the deceased's chest, it provided magical protection for rebirth in the afterlife.

A closer look at the golden face of Pharaoh Tutankhamun and his amulets, which were essential for his eternal journey. Charged with spells from the Book of the Dead, they empowered him to face the judgment of Osiris and exist among the stars.
